Most courses die the same deaths: 100 hours recorded before anyone validated the topic would sell, braindump curricula students abandon at lesson 3, production perfectionism that delays launch by a year, and a finished course with no audience, no funnel, and no sales. CourseForge turns your AI agent (Cursor, Claude Code, any MCP client) into a **senior knowledge-business strategist** that engineers topic validation, curriculum design, fast production, and the launch and sales systems that turn expertise into income — end to end.

## 🛠️ Tools (5)

| Tool | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| `autonomous_course_business_architect` | Full course business strategy in one call: honest sellability read (paid-problem test, transformation clarity, provability), format selection (mini / flagship / cohort / membership), platform economics (own-site vs marketplace), stage diagnosis, and a 90-day roadmap from expertise to paying students |
| `validate_course_topic_positioning` | Validates the course will sell BEFORE recording: demand mining (reading real willingness-to-pay), competitor course archaeology (1-3 star reviews as a positioning map), the presale protocol (founding cohort seats from an outline — the validation that cannot lie), and positioning architecture (transformation statements, the narrowing paradox, angle selection) |
| `design_curriculum_learning_outcomes` | Curriculum students finish: outcome-first mapping (the anti-braindump method), the 10-minute lesson law with quick-win placement, capstone projects threaded across modules, completion engineering against the drop-off cliffs, and depth calibration from mini course to flagship to live cohort |
| `produce_course_content_fast` | Professional content at solo speed: the audio-first law and no-studio video standards, the outline-not-script recording workflow with the two-take rule and batch days, workbooks and templates that multiply perceived value, slide discipline, and the full pipeline calibrated to phone-and-free-tools or a modest kit |
| `launch_sell_scale_course` | Sells and scales: transformation-based pricing with 3-tier offer architecture and honest scarcity, the 2-week launch campaign (close day is 40-50% of revenue), the evergreen funnel that sells honestly while you sleep, audience-stage playbooks (zero audience / small list / established), and the ladder path to a knowledge business |
